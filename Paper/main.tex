\begin{abstract}
Predicting multispecies microbial community assembly and responses to environmental interventions remains a fundamental challenge in microbiome engineering. Here, we present a latent, low-dimensional consumer-resource model that projects multispecies interactions onto effective metabolic resource coordinates. Across diverse synthetic gut and livestock microbiomes, our framework accurately forecasts combinatorial assembly, enables zero-shot generalization across structured microenvironments, and predicts host-specific intervention outcomes.
\end{abstract}
